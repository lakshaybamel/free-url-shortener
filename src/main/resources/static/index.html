The story

The story behind the bite

BiteU started with a simple annoyance: URLs are too long, and nobody has time to paste, or read, an entire paragraph of link. So BiteU takes a bite out of it — literally.

Paste any link and BiteU hands back a short, shareable one in an instant, complete with a scannable QR code. No account, no tracking dashboard, no clutter. Just a small link doing a big job.

Good to know

Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account to use BiteU?

Nope. Paste your URL, hit "Bite it!", and your short link is ready. No signup required.

Can I pick my own link name?

Yes. Add anything you like in the custom alias field, and BiteU will use it if it's available.

Can I get a QR code for my link?

Every shortened link comes with a QR code automatically, ready to scan or download as a PNG.

Is BiteU free to use?

Yes, completely. No hidden costs, no premium tier — just paste and go.
